About Truth

What the app is, who makes it and how it is funded.

What Truth is

Truth: Food & Label Scanner is an Android app on Google Play (package com.foodscanrate.app). It scans barcodes and shows nutrition and processing scores using data from Open Food Facts. Photos users add are published to Open Food Facts.

Who makes it

Truth is published under the name FreeLabs Studio, which is operated by PASALA JAYANTH, an individual developer based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. FreeLabs Studio is not a registered company.

To get in touch, see the contact page.

How it's funded

Truth is a free app supported by ads from Google AdMob. The privacy policy explains how ads and data work.
