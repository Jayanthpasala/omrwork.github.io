Delete your account

You can delete your Truth account and everything backed up with it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Truth and tap the person icon (You).
  2. In the account card with your name at the top, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and backup are deleted straight away, and you are removed from your step rooms.

Without the app

Email omrwork41@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Truth account". We delete your account and backup within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Truth account (name, email address and account ID from Google or Apple sign-in).
  • Your backup: profile and goal, food and water log, weigh-ins, walks and runs with routes, daily steps, badges, scan history, settings, and, for Truth Plus members, daily phone time. (Reel counts are never backed up; they stay on your phone.)
  • Your line in every step room you joined. Rooms you made and were alone in are deleted.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Data on your own phone stays until you clear it in the app or uninstall Truth. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store: cancel them there.
