What Truth shows

One scan. The whole story.

A verdict you can read in a glance.

Nutri-Score, NOVA processing level and sugar become one plain answer: Better choice, Moderate or Enjoy occasionally. The reasons are always shown.

Medicines, down to the batch.

Reads the QR or Data Matrix for batch, manufacture and expiry, or point the camera at the printed dates. Expired packs turn red, and Truth can remind you a week before.

Ingredients, explained.

E-numbers and additives spelled out in plain words, with allergens highlighted, for food, supplements and cosmetics.

Private by design.

No account, no login. Your scan history lives only on your phone, and you can delete one scan or many at once. Barcodes go to Open Food Facts to look products up. Photos leave your phone only to read hard-to-read print, or when you choose to add a product.

Missing? Add it in a minute.

If a barcode isn't in the database yet, photograph the pack and publish it to Open Food Facts from inside Truth. You check every detail first, and the next person who scans it finds it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Truth free?

Yes. Truth is free on Google Play, with no purchases or subscriptions. It is supported by ads.

Where does the data come from?

From Open Food Facts, a non-profit open database built by people around the world. It can be incomplete or out of date, so always check the pack.

Does it work on Indian products?

Yes, if the product is in Open Food Facts. Many are, but not all. If yours is missing, you can add it with a few photos from inside Truth.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep the app free. The privacy policy explains what they collect and the choices you have.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login, and your scan history stays on your phone.

Is this medical advice?

No. Ratings and product details are general information only. Always check the packaging, especially for allergies, and ask a doctor or pharmacist about your health.
