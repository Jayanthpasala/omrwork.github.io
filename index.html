How it works

Point. Scan. Know.

01

Point the camera

At a barcode, QR code or Data Matrix. No camera handy? Type the number.

02

Get the verdict

Rating, nutrients, ingredients and pack dates arrive in about a second, straight from the open Open Food Facts database.

03

Come back to it

Every scan is saved on your phone. Filter by type, swipe one away, or select many and delete them together.
