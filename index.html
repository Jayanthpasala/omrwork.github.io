FAQ

Good questions.

Is Truth free?

Yes. Almost everything in Truth is free and stays free, supported by ads. Truth Plus is optional: it adds small extras like Progress, more buddies and no ads, and it helps us keep Truth running with servers, fixes and new features. You never need it to use Truth, and you can cancel it any time in Google Play.

Is Truth on iPhone?

Not yet. The iPhone version is on its way to the App Store. Truth is available on Android through Google Play today.

Do I need an account?

On Android, you sign in once with Google, so your progress is backed up and comes back on a new phone. On iPhone, signing in will be optional. Either way, what you log stays on your phone first, and you can delete your account and backup any time in the app or on the account deletion page.

Where does the food data come from?

Packaged foods come from Open Food Facts, a non-profit open database built by people around the world. It can be incomplete or out of date, so always check the pack. If a product is missing, you can add it with a few photos from inside Truth.

How does the reels counter work?

On Android, Truth uses an accessibility service you switch on yourself to count reels and shorts in Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and Snapchat. It only notices when a full-screen video list moves to the next video, so it can count; it doesn't read your messages or what you type. The privacy policy explains it in full.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep the app free. Truth Plus removes them. The privacy policy explains what ads collect and the choices you have.

Is this medical advice?

No. Calories, steps, scores and forecasts are estimates to guide everyday choices. Always check the packaging, especially for allergies, and ask a doctor about your health.
